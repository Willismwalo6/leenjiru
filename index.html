Welcome

A calm retreat in the heart of Nakuru

Lee Njiru Resort is a place to slow down. Whether you are planning a weekend away, a family holiday, a celebration or a working retreat, our team looks after every detail so you can simply enjoy your time here.

Wake up among green lawns and flowering hedges, spend the afternoon by the pool, share a meal in our restaurant and end the day around a campfire or at the bar. Everything you need is in one place.

  • Restful accommodation

    Suites, rooms and self-contained apartments for couples, families and groups.

  • Space to unwind

    Open gardens, a swimming pool and quiet corners to settle into.

  • Genuine hospitality

    Warm, attentive service from arrival to departure.
